## Follow-up tests (2026-11-25)

**LAG is a data artifact.** The minute prices from `prices-history` were 29-48 s old at each decision
(10th-90th percentile). Giving the rule BTC information just 1 minute older than the price turned
+18.0c into -8.1c per $1 (2 min: -7.3c, 3 min: -8.2c). Re-tested on the executable best ask/bid the round
recorder saved every few seconds for 329 live 5-minute rounds (2026-11-23..25; `research/pm_lag_recorded.py`):
273 bets, 34.8% won, **-18.2c per $1** with a 2 s fill delay (-17.8c at 5 s), worse than coin-flip sides.

**The bigger trend does not call the next 5-minute round** (`research/pm_trend_5m.py`, 11,767 5-minute and
6,431 15-minute rounds): the next 5-minute round matched the previous one 49.8% of the time. Following the
last round: -6.0c per $1 (coin flip -6.0c). Following the 15-minute window's direction: 48.3% won, -8.1c.
Following a strong (>= 70%) 15-minute favourite: 47.1% won, -10.1c.

**"Minute-4 cheap side" (25-40c) failed out of sample.** It looked consistent in the 5-minute band scan
(2026-11-11..25, `docs/PM5M_PRICE_BANDS.md`). Pre-registered and tested only on older rounds
(2026-10-11..11-10): 1,484 bets, 33.1% won at 33.2c, **-4.6c per $1**, 95% CI -11.6c to +2.5c. Not an edge.
