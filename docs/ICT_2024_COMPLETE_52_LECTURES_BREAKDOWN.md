### The Core Axiom:
Markets are not driven by retail buying/selling pressure or classic supply and demand mechanics. Instead:
1. **Central Bank Algorithms:** Price is delivered by an automated digital algorithm designed by central banking institutions.
2. **Predictable Delivery Clocks:** The algorithm operates on precise Greenwich Mean Time / New York Eastern Standard Time clocks.
3. **Liquidity Engineering:** Price exists to pair orders. When the algorithm needs to buy, it drives price into resting sell stops (Sell-Side Liquidity). When it needs to sell, it drives price into resting buy stops (Buy-Side Liquidity).
4. **Equilibrium & Imbalance:** The algorithm oscillates between offering Fair Value (rebalancing inefficiencies like Fair Value Gaps) and seeking External Liquidity (previous highs and lows).

### Phase 2: Structural Displacement & Imbalance Geometry (Lectures 16 – 25)
* **Lecture 16 – 20: Market Structure Shift (MSS) vs. Market Structure Break (MSB)**
  * A true shift in market structure requires **Displacement**: large, energetic candle bodies with minimal wicks that create an imbalance.
  * Minor pullbacks without displacement are ignored.
  * **Swing High / Swing Low Geometry:** A 3-candle fractal structure where the middle candle is flanked by lower highs (for swing highs) or higher lows (for swing lows).
* **Lecture 21 – 25: Fair Value Gaps (FVG) and Volume Imbalances**
  * **BISI (Buy-Side Imbalance, Sell-Side Inefficiency):** A 3-candle sequence where Candle 3's low is strictly higher than Candle 1's high. Sellers were denied access; price must return to rebalance.
  * **SIBI (Sell-Side Imbalance, Buy-Side Inefficiency):** A 3-candle sequence where Candle 3's high is strictly lower than Candle 1's low. Buyers were denied access; price must return to rebalance.
  * **Consequent Encroachment (50% C.E.):** The mathematical midpoint of an FVG. This is the institutional defense threshold.
